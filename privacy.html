1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal information from our users through our public website. Our TikTok integration only uses the necessary OAuth scopes to publish content to your account as authorized by you.

The access tokens provided through TikTok OAuth are used solely for the purpose of uploading and publishing music videos generated by our automated pipeline to your TikTok account.

Our service uses the TikTok Content Posting API. By using our service, you are also bound by TikTok's Privacy Policy. We do not share your data with any other third parties.
